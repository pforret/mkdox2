# Hide ai_employee, committee and dev_project from the template menu; keep them usable with -B

## Context
Planning the template-menu item (request: "default template should be: simple site, then site + blog, then Karpathy wiki and that's it"). The menu listed all six folders in `templates/`; the question was what to do with the three not in the requested menu.

## Options considered
- Hide from the menu, keep `-B <name>`: no content lost, short menu; the hidden ones are only findable by reading `templates/`.
- Delete the folders: least to maintain; `-B` for them stops working and the content is gone.
- Add a 4th 'other templates…' entry: discoverable, but the menu is no longer just three.

## Decision
Hide ai_employee, committee and dev_project from the template menu; keep them usable with -B

## Consequences
The menu shows only `simple`, `with_blog`, `karpathy_wiki`. `ai_employee`, `committee` and `dev_project` stay maintained in `templates/` and must keep working with `-B`. Any new template is hidden until it is added to the menu array (see decisions/2026-11-01.template-menu-order-is-a-hardcoded.md).
