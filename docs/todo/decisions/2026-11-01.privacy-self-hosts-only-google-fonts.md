# privacy self-hosts only Google Fonts and the glightbox assets (assets_include)

## Context
With `[project.plugins.privacy]` and no settings, a 4-page site got 7.7 MB in `site/assets/external` (90% of the site): it self-hosts every external URL in Zensical's theme bundle, including Pyodide and the Ace editor (6.2 MB from unpkg), Google ad/IMA scripts and Vimeo for the video player. Fonts were only 0.5 MB. The PRP didn't cover this; the user chose during implementation.

## Options considered
- `assets_include` = Google Fonts + glightbox: about 1 MB, keeps the GDPR win; other assets stay remote and only load when a page uses them.
- `assets_exclude` for the heavy ones: self-hosts the rest; the list needs updating when the theme adds new CDNs.
- Keep everything: 7.7 MB per site, no third-party request ever.

## Decision
privacy self-hosts only Google Fonts and the glightbox assets (assets_include)

## Consequences
All 6 templates have `assets_include = ["fonts.googleapis.com/*", "fonts.gstatic.com/*", "unpkg.com/glightbox@*"]`. Sites are 1.4–2.6 MB. Mermaid (used by the superfences config) and the video player still load from their CDNs; add patterns to self-host them.
